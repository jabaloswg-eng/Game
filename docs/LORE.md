# EMBERVEIL — Lore Bible

The written canon behind `js/data/lore.js`. Warm high fantasy, a little wry, never grimdark.

## The Veil and the Maelstrom

The world of Emberveil floats in the **Maelstrom** — an endless ocean of raw, unshaped mana. The Maelstrom is not malevolent; it simply has no notion of how small living things are. Whatever it touches, it remakes.

For a thousand years the world was sealed from it by the **Veil**, a living curtain of woven light raised in an age no chronicle remembers. While it held whole, mana fell only as a gentle rain: hearth-charms, harvest-blessings, small polite magics. The eldest texts insist the Veil is not a thing but a being — and that it dreams.

## The Sundering and the Hollow King

The Archmage **Vael Morwyn**, wisest of his age and terrified of dying like everyone else, cut the Veil open above his sanctum to drink the Maelstrom raw. The sky burned for nine nights — **the Sundering**, the night the old calendar ended.

Morwyn got exactly what he asked for, which is the cruelest thing magic can do. The storm burned away his death — and with it his warmth, his loves, and finally his name. What remained crowned itself the **Hollow King** and descended into the deep places beneath his ruined sanctum, raising an **Umbral Legion** from the loyal dead. Even a hollow thing remembers being whole: he hunts the shards of the Veil to swallow their light.

To hold the breach, the Veil shattered its own heart into five **Veilshards**, flung across the land like embers from a struck fire. Wherever one fell, the land grew strange, and some creature swollen on its power now guards it jealously.

## Lumenhold and the Veilwalkers

The free city of **Lumenhold** survived the burning sky by lamplight: thousands of lanterns hung on its walls in one desperate night, each fed a drop of Veil-light. Monsters will not cross that glow, and the lantern-wardens let no flame gutter. Lumenhold is now hearth and haven to all Emberveil — markets loud, bank famously secure, blacksmith famously optimistic. **Elder Maren** keeps the city's chronicle and watches for children born with the spark.

Children born after the Sundering sometimes carry a splinter of the Veil in their blood. These **Veilwalkers** can stand in raw mana unchanged and close the wounds in the world — which is why they are sent toward every danger sensible people flee. The player is the newest of them.

### The Three Orders

Three orders train Veilwalkers, share the duty, and bicker about it warmly:

- **The Bastion Oath** (Warrior) — shieldbearers and blade-masters sworn to stand where others cannot, and not move. Led in Lumenhold by Captain Aldric.
- **The Thornwood Wardens** (Archer) — hunters raised under the old rule: a Warden may miss once, never twice. Sylvie speaks for them in the city.
- **The Azure Arcanum** (Mage) — scholars of the ember-flame, the art of asking the Maelstrom nicely and surviving the answer. Magister Orin teaches, and extinguishes.

## The Five Veilshards

Each shard fell into one zone and is held by its boss. Sealing all five (quest items `q_veilshard_1..5`) closes the wound Morwyn tore.

| Shard | Zone | Keeper | The land around it |
|---|---|---|---|
| First Shard (`q_veilshard_1`) | Dawnmeadow Fields | **Bramblehide the Sowking** (`bramblehide`) | Fell among the grain; the harvest grew huge and the boars grew huger. Bramblehide rooted it up and wears it in his hide like a burr he refuses to lose. |
| Second Shard (`q_veilshard_2`) | The Thornwood | **Oakenheart** (`oakenheart`) | Sank into the roots of the eldest tree, which woke up ancient, mighty, and extremely unwilling to share. The forest's anger radiates from Oakenheart's grove. |
| Third Shard (`q_veilshard_3`) | The Cinderdunes | **Sirocco, Broodmother of Ash** (`sirocco`) | Its heat feeds the endless mana-storm that turned the desert to ash. Sirocco nests atop it, and her brood ride the storm it breathes. |
| Fourth Shard (`q_veilshard_4`) | Frostfell Pass | **Borealis the Pale Drake** (`borealis`) | Froze the pass into a winter that never ends. Borealis hoards it as the one treasure colder and brighter than she is. |
| Fifth Shard (`q_veilshard_5`) | The Hollow Depths | **The Hollow King** (`hollow_king`) | The shard that fell nearest the breach — Morwyn seized it the night of the Sundering, and it is the last warm thing he owns. He will not part with it while anything of him remains. |

## Story arc (main quest chain)

Dawnmeadow chores for Elder Maren → Bramblehide and the First Shard → Thornwood bandit trouble → Oakenheart and the Second Shard → guarding Rasha's caravan through the Cinderdunes → Sirocco and the Third Shard → Ranger Eydis and the beacons of Frostfell → Borealis and the Fourth Shard → down into the Hollow Depths, past Lysander's shade, to face the Hollow King and carry all five shards home. The Veil dreams whole again — and presumably dreams of the Veilwalker who mended it.
